Before you sign anything

What actually drives the scope.

Four things determine a Florida re-roof scope. None of them appear on a round-number quote.

The 25% rule
Under the Florida Building Code, once repairs or replacement affect more than 25% of the roof area within any twelve-month period, the entire roof system generally has to be brought to current code — not just the section being worked on. This is the single most common reason a "repair" quote and a "replacement" quote differ by more than the labor.
Secondary water barrier
Re-roofs in high-wind areas require a secondary water barrier over the sheathing, plus re-nailing the deck to current fastening schedules. Both are hidden work that never shows in a finished photo and both are non-negotiable at inspection.
Material by exposure
Shingle is the lowest first cost and the shortest service life. Tile handles sun and salt well and carries real structural weight, which matters on an older frame. Standing-seam metal costs the most up front and lasts the longest. On a coastal parcel the honest comparison is cost per year of service, not cost per square.
Deck condition
Nobody can price sheathing replacement before tear-off, so any quote that pretends otherwise is guessing. We write deck repair as a defined unit rate in the contract so if we find rot, you already know the number instead of getting a change order.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do you do repairs, or only full replacement?

Both. But if a repair would touch more than a quarter of the roof, the 25% rule usually makes full replacement the better value — we will tell you which side of that line your roof falls on before you commit.

Metal, tile or shingle for a Sarasota home?

Depends on frame, exposure and how long you plan to hold the property. On a coastal parcel with a sound structure, metal or tile is usually the better long-run choice. Inland and short-hold, shingle is often right. We quote more than one option rather than steering you.

How long does a re-roof take?

Most single-family re-roofs run several days of active work, weather permitting, plus permit and inspection time on either end. We give a dated schedule in the contract, not a range.

Who pulls the roofing permit?

We do, as contractor of record, in whichever jurisdiction your parcel sits in — and we carry it through final inspection.
