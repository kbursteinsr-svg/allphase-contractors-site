Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how All Phase Contractors, Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") and our authorized marketing partners collect, use, and share information in connection with our website (allphasecontractorsfl.com) and the advertising we run through Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and other platforms.

Information We Collect

We collect two categories of information:

  • Information you give us. When you submit an estimate request or contact form on our site, we collect the fields you provide — typically your name, phone number, email address, city or area, project address (if provided), and a description of the work you're interested in.
  • Information collected automatically. When you visit our site, we and our advertising partners may automatically collect standard web analytics data — pages visited, timestamps, referring URL, general device and browser type, and an anonymous identifier used for advertising measurement.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiry, schedule an inspection or estimate, and provide the services you requested.
  • To follow up on quotes and answer questions about your project.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising and improve which types of homeowners we reach.
  • To comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and Analytics — Meta Pixel and Conversions API

Our website uses the Meta Pixel and the Meta Conversions API to measure the effectiveness of our Facebook and Instagram advertising. These tools send Meta information about the actions you take on our site — such as visiting a landing page or submitting a lead form — so that Meta can attribute those actions to the ads that brought you here and help us reach similar homeowners.

Personal identifiers we send to Meta (such as email address or phone number) are hashed before transmission using SHA-256 in accordance with Meta's requirements, so Meta receives them in a non-readable form used only for matching. You can learn more about Meta's data practices in the Meta Privacy Policy, and you can control how Meta uses information about you for ads through Meta's Ad Preferences.

How Our Marketing Automation Works

All Phase Contractors works with KB Digital Marketing, our digital marketing partner, to run and measure our advertising. KB Digital operates a marketing-automation application ("KB Digital Ads API") that accesses our Meta Business Portfolio strictly to manage our ad account, our pixel, and our advertising performance data on our behalf. The application does not access personal information about you beyond what is described in this policy, and it does not share your information with any party other than Meta for the purposes described above.

How We Share Information

We share your information only in the limited circumstances below:

  • Service providers that help us operate our business — for example, our marketing partner (KB Digital Marketing), our project management and quoting tools, our email and SMS delivery providers, and our website hosting provider — all under written agreements that limit their use of your information to providing services to us.
  • Advertising platforms (such as Meta) for the measurement and optimization purposes described above.
  • Legal or safety requirements when we are required to do so by law, subpoena, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of All Phase Contractors, our customers, or others.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep lead-form submissions and related project information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to your inquiry, complete any work you engage us for, and comply with our record-keeping obligations. If you'd like your information removed sooner, contact us using the details below.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of our follow-up: reply "STOP" to any text message we send, or ask us to remove you by phone or email using the contact information below.
  • Adjust ad tracking in your browser: most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies or use private browsing to limit tracking.
  • Adjust ad preferences on Meta: use Meta Ad Preferences to control how Facebook and Instagram use information about you for ads.
  • Request access, correction, or deletion: depending on where you live, you may have the right to request a copy of the information we hold about you, correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below and we will respond in accordance with applicable law.

Children

Our website and services are directed to homeowners and property owners, not to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
