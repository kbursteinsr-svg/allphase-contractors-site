Which system belongs on your house.

Different roofs, different answers. Here's how we usually think about it for Sarasota-area homes — and we'll tell you straight which one fits yours.

Longest life

Standing-Seam Metal

Concealed fasteners, no exposed screws to back out over time. Sheds rain fast, handles wind well, and reflects a lot of the summer heat load. The usual choice for waterfront and near-water homes.

Architectural match

Concrete & Clay Tile

The right answer when the neighborhood or the HOA expects it, and on Mediterranean and Spanish-style homes. Very long service life — most tile failures are really underlayment failures, which is where the install matters most.

Best value

Architectural Shingle

Still a solid system when the budget needs to work and the exposure allows it. We use high-wind-rated products and a full sealed underlayment rather than the builder-grade minimum.
