Metal vs. the alternatives.

Standing seam

Best for most homes

Concealed fasteners, nothing to back out over time. Sheds rain fast and handles wind well. The default recommendation for most Sarasota-area roofs.

Stone-coated steel

Looks like tile or shake

Metal's durability with the profile of traditional tile or wood shake — a fit when the neighborhood or HOA expects a particular look.

Asphalt shingle

Shortest lifespan

Typically 15–25 years in Florida's sun and salt air, versus 40–70 for metal. Lower upfront cost, more replacements over the life of the home.
